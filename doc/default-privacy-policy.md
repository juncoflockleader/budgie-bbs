# Default Privacy Policy

Date: 2026-06-14

This is the default privacy policy template for Budgie BBS deployments. Site operators should review and customize it before public launch.

## Information We Collect

During registration, the site may collect:

- username
- password hash
- email address
- real name or submitted name
- school, affiliation, or similar eligibility information
- registration note or reason for joining
- IP address and request metadata used for abuse prevention
- captcha verification result when captcha is enabled

After signup, the site may collect:

- posts, replies, reactions, votes, mail, direct messages, and uploaded attachments
- login/logout timestamps
- presence and online-status data
- moderation reports and administrative review notes
- profile fields that users choose to provide

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- create and secure accounts
- verify registration eligibility
- reduce spam and abuse
- provide forum, chat, mail, notification, and moderation features
- investigate safety, security, and policy issues
- maintain audit records for administrator and moderator actions

## Who Can Access Registration Information

Registration details are private administrative data. Site administrators may review them for account approval, abuse prevention, support, and security work. Registration details are not intended to appear in public profiles or public board posts.

Moderators should only receive access to private registration data if the site operator explicitly grants that access for a defined operational need.

## Email Verification And Security

Email addresses may be used for account verification, password recovery, account security notices, and optional two-factor authentication. The site should not publish user email addresses without user consent.

Passwords are stored as hashes, not plaintext. Operators should keep server secrets, mail credentials, captcha secrets, and database backups protected.

## Retention

Operators should retain registration and audit records only as long as needed for community safety, legal compliance, and operational continuity. Users may request account deactivation or removal according to site policy.

## Disclosure

Operators should not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law, to protect the site or its users, or to investigate abuse and security incidents.

## Updates

Operators may update this policy as features and legal requirements change. Material changes should be announced where users can reasonably see them.

## Contact

Site operators should publish a contact method for privacy, safety, and account questions.
